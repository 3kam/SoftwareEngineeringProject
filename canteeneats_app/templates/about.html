About Our Canteen

Operational frameworks and nutrition guidelines for St Marys Senior High School.

Daily Operations

Our system processes online pre-ordered trays for both Recess and Lunch pick-up windows. Cut-off times apply strictly at the start of the school day to ensure kitchen prep timelines are maintained.

Healthy School Canteen Strategy

Our complete relational SQLite product index matches the NSW Healthy School Canteens Strategy guidelines, classifying items cleanly by operational frequency:

  • Everyday Items (V / G): High-nutrition essentials available to order without restriction.
  • Occasional Items: Secondary options structured to manage student balance volumes safely.
